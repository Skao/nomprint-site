Nomprint

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Nomprint is a personal food-memory app. It has no user accounts and no server — everything you enter lives on your own device. This page explains exactly what the app does with the permissions it asks for.

What Nomprint stores

Venue names, photos, notes, ratings, location data, and the places on your To Try list (including who recommended them) are saved locally in the app's private storage on your device. Nomprint does not transmit this data to any server — there isn't one. Nothing is collected, analyzed, or shared by the app's developer.

Permissions Nomprint uses

  • Camera — to let you take a photo when adding a nom. Photos are saved only on your device.
  • Photo library — to let you attach an existing photo instead of taking a new one.
  • Location — used once when you add an entry, to tag it with where you ate and show it on the map later. Location is only read on-device; it is not sent anywhere.

Maps

The map view is rendered using Google's Maps SDK, and addresses are resolved using your device's built-in location services. Map display and on-device geocoding are handled locally by the platform (Google/Apple) and are not routed through any server operated by Nomprint's developer. Google's own privacy policy governs its map rendering service.

Backing up, restoring, and sharing

Backup/Restore (in the menu) lets you save all your entries, photos, categories, and To Try list as a single file — either into a folder you choose on your device, or sent to another app such as Google Drive or email. Restore reads a backup file you choose back into the app. Share Map lets you send a snapshot of the map. These are all actions you explicitly trigger, and a file only goes where you send it — the app doesn't send anything anywhere on its own. A backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Purchases

Nomprint is free for up to 10 entries. Nomprint Unlimited is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play or the App Store. Your payment details go to the store, never to Nomprint, and the store's own privacy policy covers the purchase. When the app starts, and when you tap Restore purchase, it asks the store on your device whether your store account owns Nomprint Unlimited. That yes-or-no answer is the only purchase information the app keeps, and it stays on your device.

No accounts, no tracking

Nomprint has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
